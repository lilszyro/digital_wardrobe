# Digital Fashion Creation

This context defines the language used for creating and presenting original clothing ideas. It distinguishes an expressive visual design from the technical information needed to construct a real garment.

## Language

**Visual Design Concept**:
A saved representation of how an imagined garment should look, composed from a garment shape and visual choices such as colour, material, pattern, supported details, and notes. It is not guaranteed to contain enough technical information to construct the garment.
_Avoid_: Production pattern, technical specification, finished garment

**Garment Shape**:
The editable starting form that establishes the type and broad silhouette of a Visual Design Concept, such as a shirt, dress, or trousers.
_Avoid_: Sewing pattern, template

**Garment Piece**:
The main garment body or any separate piece created when Cutting divides a Garment Shape. A Visual Design Concept may contain one or more Garment Pieces; each disconnected piece can be moved and rotated independently.
_Avoid_: Waste, automatically discarded fragment

**Construction Guidance**:
Future information intended to help someone turn a Visual Design Concept into a physical garment. It may eventually include steps, measurements, or patterns, but it is outside the first version's committed scope.
_Avoid_: Visual design, guaranteed instructions

**New Designer**:
A person without professional fashion-design training who wants an approachable way to express a clothing idea visually. The website does not require them to understand professional design software or terminology.
_Avoid_: Professional designer, manufacturer

**Garment Reshaping**:
Personalising an existing Garment Shape by removing any selected portion through Cutting.
_Avoid_: Freehand drawing, surface decoration

**Cutting**:
A reversible editing action in which a user traces a closed lasso, previews the selected area, and confirms its removal from anywhere on a Garment Shape. It is not restricted to predefined garment areas.
_Avoid_: Trimming presets, freehand decoration

## Flagged ambiguities

**Material**:
The current idea may use “material” to mean either a visual texture applied to a design or a real fabric with physical properties. This distinction has not yet been resolved.


## Example dialogue

> **Creator:** I applied a floral material to a dress shape and saved the result.
>
> **Product expert:** You have completed a Visual Design Concept. It shows your intended appearance, but it does not yet provide Construction Guidance for making the dress.
